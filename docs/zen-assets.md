# Serein asset sources

The rebuilt world combines original Perlin-noise terrain, instanced alien ground cover, branching woodland, a shaded procedural sky, and a rigged astronaut with authored animation with locally hosted CC0 models. Astroneer and No Man’s Sky are visual/exploration references only; no assets from those games are included.

## Quaternius Ultimate Space Kit — included

- Author: Quaternius
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatespacekit.html
- Creator-linked download: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17F8HlI2zPTlo32aieW5YPPwOk78xo-2m
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Local files: `public/zen/quaternius/`
- Included models: `GeodesicDome`, `House_Cylinder`, and an adapted `Astronaut_RaeTheRedPanda` rig (`explorer.glb`).
- The self-contained glTF files were converted to binary GLB, preserving their authored meshes, materials and embedded atlas textures.
- The earlier plant files remain available locally but are no longer placed in the world. The greenhouse and workshop use the dome and cylindrical habitat.
- Attribution and source record: `public/zen/quaternius/LICENSE.txt`.

## Kenney Nature Kit — included

- Author: Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/nature-kit/37ac38a37b-1677698939/kenney_nature-kit.zip
- License: CC0; original license included at `public/zen/nature/LICENSE.txt`.
- Local models: `public/zen/nature/`; oak, branching deciduous, and tall pine models populate the woodland and ridge. Additional leaf and rock assets are available locally for later expansion.

## Kenney Space Kit — included

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/space-kit/20874c75ac-1677698978/kenney_space-kit.zip
- License: CC0; original license included at `public/zen/KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
- Local models: `public/zen/models/`; spacecraft and satellite dishes represent portfolio discoveries.
- Materials are adjusted for the world’s lighting and palette.

All assets are served locally. Models are requested only when the game is launched. Sources were retrieved September 12–13, 2026.

The character retains the CC0 suit skeleton and Idle/Walk/Run/Jump/Jump_Idle/Jump_Land clips. The animal head triangles are removed at load time and replaced with an opaque EVA helmet and visor; the pistol is removed. The life-support pack and twin jet effects are original procedural geometry attached to the animated torso.
